Pre SpaceX PreIPO Campaign

Project Introduction

SpaceX (Space Exploration Technologies Corp.) was founded by Elon Musk in 2002. It is dedicated to reducing satellite launch costs through reusable rockets and advancing commercial spaceflight. Its representative products include the Falcon 9, Dragon spacecraft, and Starship, with the long-term goal of enabling human colonization of Mars and multi-planetary existence. Pre SpaceX is a mapped token for assets issued by the regulated issuer DigiFT, designed to mirror the economic returns of SpaceX after its IPO.

How does IPO Prime work?

Just like previously issued IPO subscription assets on Deshare. The more you invest, the larger your allocated share!

Each user's investment amount cannot exceed their personal investment limit.

How are fees and carry charged?

The fee includes the subscription fee and the management fee of the underlying fund, which are collected uniformly at the time of purchase. Carry refers to the share of profits collected by the fund, which is charged by the underlying fund of the asset.

How to trade and settle preSPAX?

After the IPO Prime token distribution ends, tokens can be freely transferred and traded over-the-counter according to the rules.

Additionally, you may wait for the lock-up period of the underlying debt assets to end (usually about 6 months after the SpaceX IPO). The issuer will delegate Bitget to redeem the tokens for equity tokens or USDT for users proportionally, using the target company's stock market price as a reference and deducting the carry funds. Please refer to the subscription agreement for specific details.
