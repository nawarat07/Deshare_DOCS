Project Overview

SpaceX (Space Exploration Technologies Corp.) was founded by Elon Musk in 2002 and has become the undisputed global leader in commercial spaceflight. Its core competitive advantage lies in its proprietary reusable rocket technology, which has fundamentally reduced launch costs and accelerated humanity's access to space.

SpaceX's core product portfolio includes:

  • Falcon 9: The world's first orbital-class rocket to achieve first-stage booster recovery and reuse. It has completed hundreds of commercial missions, providing reliable launch services for both government and commercial customers.
  • Dragon: The primary spacecraft for NASA's International Space Station cargo and crewed missions. It is currently the only active commercial spacecraft capable of both crewed and cargo operations.
  • Starship: The next-generation fully reusable heavy-lift launch system currently under development. Designed to support lunar base construction and crewed Mars colonization, it is regarded as critical infrastructure for making humanity a multi-planetary species.
  • Starlink: The world's largest satellite internet constellation, providing high-speed, low-latency internet access to underserved regions globally through thousands of low-Earth orbit satellites. It already serves millions of active subscribers.

How does PreIPO work?

PreIPO follows the same proven mechanism as previously issued IPO subscription assets on DeShare. The core principle is straightforward: the more you invest, the larger your allocated share.

1

Connect Wallet & Prepare Funds

Connect your Web3 wallet (e.g., MetaMask, Rabby, WalletConnect) and ensure it holds sufficient USDT or USDC stablecoins for subscription.

2

Submit Subscription

Select your desired subscription amount on the campaign page and confirm the transaction. DeShare's audited smart contract will automatically lock your stablecoins and process the allocation. Each user's investment is subject to individual investment limits.

3

Token Allocation

Like previous PreIPO assets on DeShare, the system allocates tokens to users through a lottery system. Any unallocated portion, along with the subscription fee, will be directly refunded to your original wallet.

4

Hold or Trade

Successfully allocated PreSPX tokens can be held in your wallet to await final settlement, or traded on the OTC market that opens following the distribution period.

Trading & Settlement

The complete lifecycle of PreSPX tokens encompasses the following stages:

Phase 1: Open Trading (Post-Distribution)

Once the PreIPO token distribution is complete, PreSPX tokens become freely transferable. Users may buy and sell PreSPX on the over-the-counter (OTC) market that opens following the allocation, subject to platform trading rules. Market prices are determined freely by supply and demand.

Phase 2: Final Settlement (Post Lock-Up Period)

After SpaceX officially goes public, the underlying debt assets are typically subject to a lock-up period of approximately 6 months. Once the lock-up period expires, the issuer will delegate Bitget to complete the final settlement according to the following terms:

  • Price Reference: The real-time market trading price of SpaceX stock on the public secondary market serves as the settlement benchmark.
  • Fee Deduction: The 15% performance carry is deducted from the settlement amount (applied only to the profit portion).
  • Settlement Form: Users may receive their proceeds proportionally in the form of equity tokens or USDT, as specified in the subscription agreement.

📄 Final settlement terms are subject to the subscription agreement signed by the user.
